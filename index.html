  • Chrome Bundesliga Hobby gives the most /50-or-lower autos per dollar (scarcity table below) and ranks sixth here. How the numbers work

  • Autos per box comes from the odds sheet, as described under Scarcity columns below.
  • Value of one auto row. Each auto row on the odds sheet (Gold /50, Orange Lava /25 and so on) gets SportsCardsPro's values for that parallel across the whole checklist. A pulled auto is a random player, so a Bundesliga Gold /50 averages over all 192 Bundesliga autograph players.
  • High is the mean over cards that have a value. SportsCardsPro gives no value to a card with no recorded sales, so the high bound skips the cards nobody buys and runs optimistic.
  • Low spreads the total value over every card on the checklist, so a card nobody bought counts as $0. You may not sell a card that nobody has bought in a year. This bound carries the demand difference between leagues, and the ranking uses it.
  • Autos whose card sold in the past year weights each auto row by the share of its checklist with at least one sale in SportsCardsPro's yearly sales-volume.
  • Autos valued is the share of a box's expected autos that fall in an auto set pulled in full. Duals, triples, Superior Signatures, Future Stars and Road to Glory are not pulled; the box value scales them at the average of the valued rows.
  • What it leaves out. Only autos count. Numbered base parallels and case-hit inserts (Anime, Bionic) add to a box's return, so the full return runs higher than this table.
  • Missing cards. SportsCardsPro does not list the Chrome UCC 2025-26 autos of Mbappé, Wirtz, Doué or Adriano (4 of 112 players). The UCC 2025-26 values leave them out, which pulls those rows down.
  • Coverage check. Players pulled against each checklist's size on Checklist Insider: Bundesliga 192, Chrome UCC 2025-26 108 of 112, Legends 65, Black Lazer 45, Chrome PL 127 of 128, Finest PL 89, Merlin PL 80, Chrome UCC 2024-25 175, Merlin UCC 2024-25 91.
  • Not valued yet: the US-sport boxes, WWE and Flagship UCC. Their auto sets have not been pulled.

Box prices and the last-5 rule

The owner's price rule: take a box's last 5 eBay sales, drop the highest and lowest, and average the other 3. No price in this file follows it yet. SportsCardsPro's API returns one current value per item ("Historic prices and historic sales are not supported", API docs read 2026-10-06), and its web pages block scripts. The soccer prices here are SportsCardsPro headline values from an unpublished method. The US-sport prices are Waxstat retailer asks. Auto values have the same limit: they are SportsCardsPro's figure, not a trimmed mean of sales.

Scarcity columns

  • Autos per box sums every auto row in the box's column of the odds sheet: per-pack odds times packs per box. For every guaranteed box but one, the sum lands between 90% and 106% of the printed auto guarantee, which checks that the parser read the right column. WWE runs 20% over its guarantee of 2; a guarantee sets a floor.
  • Print runs come from parallel colors: Gold /50, Orange /25, Black /10, Red and Frozenfractor /5, Superfractor, Padparadscha, Nightshade and printing plates 1/1. "Black & White", "Gold Ink" and "Red Ink" parallels do not count. sourced means I checked the color-to-print-run ladder against Checklist Insider's checklist for that product (2025-26 Chrome UCC, Chrome Bundesliga, both Sapphire Editions). estimate means the box uses the same color names without that check.
  • Chance of a /50-or-lower auto treats each guaranteed auto as one draw, in the tier at the share the odds sheet gives. Boxes with no guarantee (Mega, Value Blaster, Flagship UCC) use a Poisson count.
  • Chance of any 1/1 counts every one-of-one in the box, autos or not.
  • Odds sheet: "Topps" is the PDF from Topps's own CDN (fact). "Mirror" is Checklist Insider's copy (sourced); two earlier mirror files matched Topps byte for byte.
  • Not covered: these numbers measure scarcity, not player value or demand. A /50 auto of a bench player can sell for less than a box. Of the 192 Bundesliga Gold /50 autos, 98 had no sale in the past year, and the median of the rest is $12.80; Chrome PL Gold /50 autos have a median of $69.00. The resale table above covers both.
